Practical case · Incoterms® 2020 quotation · dry bulk

French milling wheat from Chartres to Settat

Chartres (Beauce) → road → Port of Rouen (grain terminal) → bulk carrier → Casablanca → road → Settat

The case

Beauce Céréales, a grain cooperative based in Chartres (28), has sold French milling wheat to Minoterie Chaouia, a flour mill located in Settat (Morocco).

The wheat travels as dry bulk: the grains are poured loose into the holds of a chartered bulk carrier. There is no packing.

Quantity sold25 000 T
Price of the wheat215,00 € per T
Exchange rate1 EUR = 10,85 MAD

Your task

  1. In bulk shipping, every cost is charged on the actual weight in tonnes.
  2. Drag each step number and each step into the right row of the quotation sheet, then drag each Incoterm onto its subtotal row.
  3. Calculate every amount. Steps in France are in EUR; steps in Morocco are in MAD. Convert the CIF price into MAD, then carry on in MAD.
  4. Round every result to the cent. Use French number format (e.g. 1 234,56).
